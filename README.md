## ◈ About LEGACY

**LEGACY** is a next-generation biometric payment solution designed to transform how merchants accept and process digital payments.

By combining **fingerprint authentication**, **Near Field Communication (NFC)** and **onchain payment infrastructure**, LEGACY explores a more intuitive way for customers to authorise transactions using their unique biometric identity.

From merchant payment terminals to compatible smartphones, LEGACY aims to connect biometric identity with modern digital payment technology.

## ✧ Key Features

- **Fingerprint Authentication** — Biometric identity verification for secure payment authorisation.
- **NFC Payments** — Contactless communication between compatible payment devices.
- **Finger-to-Machine** — A payment experience using a dedicated fingerprint reader integrated with a compatible POS terminal.
- **Finger-to-Phone** — A payment experience using a designated fingerprint reader connected to a recipient's smartphone.
- **Onchain Payments** — Blockchain-based infrastructure for digital payment processing and settlement.
- **Merchant Dashboard** — A streamlined interface for merchants to manage and monitor payment activity.
- **Transaction Management** — An organised view of payment activity and transaction information.
- **Security-Focused Design** — A biometric-first approach to payment authorisation.

## ✦ Payment Experience

LEGACY is built around two proposed biometric payment journeys:

### 1. Finger-to-Machine
A customer places their finger on a compatible fingerprint reader integrated into a merchant's payment terminal. Their biometric identity is used to authorise the payment.

### 2. Finger-to-Phone
A customer uses a designated fingerprint reader connected to a compatible smartphone to authorise a payment.

Both experiences aim to make digital payments more accessible, intuitive and secure.

## ◈ Our Vision

We envision a future where merchants can accept payments through simpler, faster and more intuitive technology.

LEGACY aims to bridge biometric identity, payment terminals and onchain financial infrastructure, creating a new payment experience for merchants and customers across Africa.
