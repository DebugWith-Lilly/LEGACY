## ◈ About LEGACY

**LEGACY** is a next-generation biometric payment solution designed to transform how merchants accept and process digital payments.

By combining **fingerprint authentication**, **Near Field Communication (NFC)** and **onchain payment infrastructure**, LEGACY introduces a new way for customers to authorise transactions using their unique biometric identity.

Whether paying at a merchant's terminal or transferring money through a compatible smartphone, LEGACY connects biometric identity with modern digital payment technology.

## ✧ Key Features

- **Fingerprint Authentication** — Verify payment authorisations using a securely registered biometric identity.
- **NFC Payments** — Enable contactless communication between compatible payment devices.
- **Finger-to-Machine** — Authorise merchant payments using a dedicated fingerprint reader integrated into a compatible POS terminal.
- **Finger-to-Phone** — Enable payments through a designated fingerprint reader connected to a recipient's smartphone.
- **Onchain Payments** — Leverage blockchain-based infrastructure for digital payment processing and transaction settlement.
- **Merchant Experience** — Provide merchants with a streamlined way to accept payments and monitor transactions.
- **Security-Focused Design** — Built around biometric verification and secure transaction authorisation.
